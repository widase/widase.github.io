Support & Contact

Need help with Widase, found an error in a reading, or have an idea? Email us at widase.app@gmail.com. We read every message and aim to reply within a few days. Widase is maintained by a small volunteer team. You can also send feedback from inside the app: More → Send feedback.

Common questions

Does Widase need an account or internet?

No. There is no sign-up, and the Bible, prayers, and calendar are bundled with the app, so it works offline. When you are online, the app quietly downloads new and corrected content.

The daily reading or a date looks wrong

Please tell us the date, the section (for example ቅዳሴ or ምስባክ), and what you expected. Report a content error. Corrections are published to the app without an app update.

Notifications or reminders don't arrive

Check that notifications are allowed for Widase in your phone's Settings, then open More → Reminders in the app and save your reminder again.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, open More → Delete account & data. This permanently removes the data linked to your install from our servers and from your phone. You can also email us a deletion request. See the Privacy Policy for details.
