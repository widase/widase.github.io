A small project, a long tradition.

Widase is a free, beautifully designed mobile companion for Orthodox prayer, scripture reading, and the calendar of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church. It is built and maintained by a small group of volunteers who wanted a tool that worked offline, treated their attention with respect, and looked beautiful while doing so.

Made with care

Every screen is hand-tuned for the eye, the heart, and the screen sizes people actually own.

Community-led

Translations and content are contributed by clergy and laity across the diaspora.

Privacy-first

No tracking, no ads, no account required. Anonymous by default.

If you would like to contribute — translation, recitation, theological review, or code — please get in touch.
