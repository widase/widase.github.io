Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-08-07.

Widase is a free devotional app. You can use it without an account — no sign-up is required, and the app works fully offline. This policy explains, in plain language, what information the app does send to our servers, why, and how you can delete it.

Who is responsible for your data

Widase is operated by a small volunteer team. For any privacy question or request, email hello@prayerapp.com.

What we collect

If you use the app without an account

  • Device record — a random per-install device ID (generated on your device, not tied to your identity), plus the device platform (iOS/Android), locale, timezone, app version, and when the device last checked in. This lets us deliver the right content and understand which app versions are in use.
  • Push token — only if you turn notifications on. Used solely to deliver the notifications you asked for.
  • Reading activity — which book or section was opened, the position reached, how long it was read, and when. We also derive simple reading statistics (streaks, sections read, days read). This powers your reading progress and helps us understand which content is useful. It is keyed to the random device ID, not to your identity.
  • Favorites, reading positions, and settings — so they can be restored when you reinstall or sync.

If you create an optional account

  • Account details — your email address, display name, a securely hashed password (we never store the password itself), your preferred language, and your account roles.
  • All of the per-device data above, associated with your account so it follows you across devices.

Other data

  • Feedback messages — if you send feedback or contact us through the app, we keep the message so we can respond.
  • Server logs — like nearly every internet service, our servers (Google Cloud Run) record request logs that include IP addresses. We use them only for security and operations, and they expire on the cloud provider's standard log-retention schedule.

What we don't do

  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • No ads, and no selling or sharing of your data with data brokers — ever.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, or microphone.
  • No cross-site or cross-app tracking identifiers.

Why we collect it

Everything above serves one of three purposes: making the app work (sync, notifications, restoring your progress), keeping it reliable and secure (logs, app-version info), or improving the content (aggregate reading statistics). We do not use your data for anything else.

How long we keep it

Account data, favorites, reading positions, statistics, and reading events are retained until you delete your account or ask us to delete the data for your device. Server request logs expire automatically on the cloud provider's standard retention schedule.

Deleting your data

In the app, Settings → Delete account & data removes your account and the server data associated with it — favorites, reading statistics, reading events, and device records. If you use the app without an account, or prefer not to use the in-app option, email hello@prayerapp.com and we will delete the data linked to your device.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what data we hold about you, ask for a copy, or ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Use the in-app deletion option or email hello@prayerapp.com.

Donations

The current version of the app does not offer in-app donations or any payments, so no payment information is collected. If donations are added in a future version, this policy will be updated first.

Children

Widase is suitable for all ages. We do not target children, show ads, or knowingly collect more data from children than from anyone else — which, without an account, is limited to the anonymous device data described above.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. Material changes will also be announced in the app.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email hello@prayerapp.com.
