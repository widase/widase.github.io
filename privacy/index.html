Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-12.

Widase is a free devotional app for the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo faith. No sign-up is required and the app works fully offline. This policy explains, in plain language, what information the app sends to our servers, why, who helps us process it, and how you can delete it. It applies to the Widase iOS and Android apps and to this website.

Summary

  • No ads, no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • We never sell or share your data with data brokers or advertisers.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera, or microphone.
  • What we do collect is keyed to a random install ID, not to your name or email.
  • You can delete it from inside the app at any time (More → Delete account & data).

Who is responsible for your data

Widase is operated by a small volunteer team. For any privacy question or request, email widase.app@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Install record: a random ID created on your device when the app is first opened, plus the platform (iOS/Android), app language, timezone, app version, and when the app last checked in. It lets the app download the right content and lets us see which app versions are still in use.
  • Reading activity: which book or section was opened, how far you read, for how long, and when. It powers your reading progress and streaks and shows us, in aggregate, which content is useful. It is tied to the random install ID only.
  • Favorites, reading positions, and settings: so they can be restored when you reinstall or move to a new phone.
  • Notification token: only if you allow notifications. Used solely to deliver the reminders and announcements you turned on. Reminders you schedule are stored on your device.
  • Feedback: if you send feedback in the app, we keep your message, its category and rating, your app version and platform, and your email address only if you choose to include one so we can reply.
  • Server logs: like nearly every internet service, our servers record request logs that include IP addresses. We use them only for security and keeping the service running.

The current version of the app does not offer sign-up, sign-in, or payments, so we do not collect your name, email address (except as above for feedback), or any payment information. If that changes, this policy will be updated before the new version is released.

How we use it

Only to make the app work (content sync, notifications, restoring your progress), to keep it reliable and secure (logs, app-version information), and to improve the content (aggregate reading statistics). We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or anything else, and we do not use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Service providers

We rely on a few providers who process data only on our behalf:

  • Google Cloud and our managed database host: run our servers and store the data described above and our request logs.
  • Expo, together with Apple Push Notification service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: deliver notifications, if you turn them on.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website.

We may also disclose information if required by law. Our servers are located in the United States, so your data may be processed outside your country.

How long we keep it

Install records, reading activity, favorites, and settings are kept until you delete them (see below). Feedback is kept for as long as needed to respond and improve the app. Server request logs expire automatically on our cloud provider's standard retention schedule (typically 30 days).

Deleting your data

In the app, open More → Delete account & data (Amharic: ተጨማሪ → መለያና ውሂብ ሰርዝ). This permanently deletes the server data linked to your install, including reading activity, statistics, favorites, and device records, and erases the app's data on your phone. You can also email widase.app@gmail.com and we will delete it for you. Uninstalling the app removes the data on your phone but not on our servers.

Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Access to our servers and database is restricted to the people who operate Widase. No system is perfectly secure, but we collect as little as possible to limit the risk.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what data we hold about you, ask for a copy, or ask for it to be corrected or deleted. We do not sell or "share" personal information as those terms are used in California law. Use the in-app deletion option or email widase.app@gmail.com; we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Widase is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them beyond the anonymous install data described above. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, such as an email address in feedback, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. Material changes will also be announced in the app.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email widase.app@gmail.com.
