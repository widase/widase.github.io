Widase is free — and will stay free.

There are no ads, no subscriptions, and no in-app purchases. Donations are not available in the current version of the app; we may add a way to contribute in the future.

How you can help today

  • Share the app with your parish, family, and friends — word of mouth is how it grows.
  • Leave a review on the App Store or Google Play — it helps others find it.
  • Send feedback — corrections to texts, translation help, and feature ideas are all welcome at widase.app@gmail.com.

Thank you for praying with us.
